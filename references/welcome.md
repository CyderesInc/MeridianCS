> connected into it: it can only see what your connectors feed it. A failed connector, or a source
> nobody onboarded, shows up not as a gap but as a smaller number.
> **Confirm your connectors are healthy before you rely on a result** — I show coverage on connect.
>
> Used outside the path this skill provides — hand-edited scripts, direct API calls, or figures
> carried in from another tool — **outcomes are not guaranteed**. What comes back is decision
> support, not a system of record and not professional advice.
>
> 👋 **Welcome to Meridian for Claude** — your Meridian (formerly Lucidum) stack, now
> queryable in plain English.
>
> **Why this is useful:** Meridian unifies every asset, user, and risk signal from your connected
> security tools into one inventory. This skill turns that inventory into answers — ask a question
> the way you'd say it out loud and get back a ranked, color-coded result in seconds, plus a
> shareable Cyderes-branded report or blast-radius graph when you need something to send on. No
> query language to learn, no clicking through the console.
>
> **Things you can ask me:**
>
> - "Who are my riskiest users right now?"
> - "Which internet-facing assets have known-exploited (KEV) vulnerabilities?"
> - "What's missing CrowdStrike or MFA?"
> - "Show me the certificates expiring in the next 30 days."
> - "Give me a blast-radius profile for `<person>` as a PDF."
>
> **To point me at your Meridian stack, I need two things:**
>
> 1. **FQDN** — your Meridian address, e.g. `company.lucidum.cloud` (no `https://`, no path).
> 2. **API token** — a *User Generated* token. In Meridian: **Settings → User Management →** your
>    account **→ Edit**, make sure the **`Api_Users`** role is assigned, then **Generate Token** and
>    copy it (Meridian shows it only once).
>
> Paste both and I'll validate and connect. **Already set up?** I'll detect your saved credentials
> automatically — no need to re-enter anything.
